# Rolling publication — November 17, 2026

The owner asked to publish news as it is seen and verified, rather than wait for a complete review and another hourly publishing slot. This explicitly replaces the former globally-best-five-before-publication policy. The source roster, hourly collection cadence, model/provider, spending ceilings and content/publication guards stay in place. One recurring pipeline schedule replaces the four stage schedules.

Committed qualifying discovery decisions can enter verification before the full scan is reviewed. One early verification per committed review batch interleaves with remaining review; a full global queue yields to background review. Up to ten existing capture pages run in a bounded invocation, with larger scans continuing from their saved cursor. Every eligible candidate is still reviewed. Obsolete global sorting and final top-five writes are removed; scan completion validates coverage and records counts. Historical audit rows remain intact.

The separate five-qualified/submitted-stories-per-parent-run ceilings are removed. Shared pending capacity remains five distinct stories, and actual publication attempts remain capped at five per account/UTC hour. Verification still retrieves public sources, validates evidence/identity/freshness/attribution and binds the full deduplication history. No model gets configuration, credential or budget authority.

Insertion into the verified agent inbox immediately dispatches the existing guarded curator; approval/preparation immediately dispatches the existing guarded publisher. A single recurring pipeline tick drives collection/review and retries durable queued work. Per-stage dispatch coalescing prevents duplicate calls within one tick; requests begin after their database transaction commits. The publisher skips authentication when its actual queue is empty. Its self-imposed login budget becomes five atomic attempts per account/UTC hour, including failed logins, preserving the already-used slot and timestamp. Bluesky's official limits at review time are 30 session creations per five minutes and 300/day; five/hour stays below both without introducing a token cache or credential store: https://bsky.network/docs/rate-limits/ .

Validation uses focused rolling-review, receipt/admission, empty-queue, multi-minute mock-HTTP publication, login counter and concurrency checks plus existing CI. Fabricated provider/PDS responses do not claim actual editorial quality or real publication. The production publisher index retains its existing enabled value when bundled; deploying the tracked disabled safety index would incorrectly stop publication.

Terminally fenced Gemma discovery runs may supply only their previously paid, committed decision prefix: their state must exactly match the immutable quarantine audit. The failed batch and unreviewed tail never become eligible, and the old workflow/attempt stays fenced with its full hold. This allows already completed useful work to proceed without replaying a failed request.
